# Production launch checklist

Use this before delivering the template to a real tattoo studio.

## 1. Brand and content
- Replace `site-config.js` values with the real studio details.
- Replace fictional artist names, biographies and specialties.
- Replace sample testimonials with verified reviews.
- Replace all demo wording that says fictional, demo or placeholder.
- Replace placeholder contact details and opening hours.

## 2. Images
- Add optimized client-owned images under `assets/`.
- Replace remote demo image URLs with local assets.
- Check every image crop on desktop and mobile.
- Create a branded social preview image.
- Confirm the client has rights to every image used.

## 3. Booking
- Replace the mailto demo form with the chosen production booking method.
- Confirm WhatsApp number and pre-filled message.
- Confirm deposit wording, rescheduling policy and cancellation policy.
- Submit at least two real test enquiries before launch.

## 4. SEO and sharing
- Update page titles and descriptions.
- Update `siteUrl` in `site-config.js` after the final domain is connected.
- Update `sitemap.xml` for the final domain.
- Confirm canonical URLs resolve correctly.
- Validate structured data after the final address/contact details are entered.
- Test Facebook/WhatsApp/LinkedIn link previews.

## 5. Performance
- Prefer WebP or AVIF for photography.
- Avoid oversized source images.
- Test the homepage on mobile data, not only Wi-Fi.
- Check for broken links and missing images.
- Test Chrome, Safari and at least one Android browser.

## 6. Accessibility and QA
- Navigate the site using only a keyboard.
- Confirm visible focus states.
- Confirm text contrast remains readable over photography.
- Check form labels and required fields.
- Test the gallery lightbox and Escape-to-close behavior.
- Test at 320 px, 375 px, 768 px, 1024 px and desktop widths.

## 7. Client handoff
- Connect the client's final domain.
- Provide the client with their GitHub/repository ownership or agreed hosting access.
- Record where contact details, artists, reviews and images are edited.
- Store original design assets separately from the production website.
- Confirm the agreed maintenance plan and what is included.

## Launch rule

Do not call a site production-ready until the real booking flow, real contact details, real imagery, final domain and mobile QA have all been completed.
