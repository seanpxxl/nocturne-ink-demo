## Audit checklist for every prospect

Before messaging, answer:

1. Do they have a website?
2. Is it mobile-friendly?
3. Is their best tattoo work easy to find?
4. Are artists individually presented?
5. Is there a clear booking CTA?
6. Is WhatsApp prominent?
7. Are pricing/deposit expectations explained?
8. Are reviews/testimonials visible?
9. Does the design match the quality of their tattoo work?
10. Is there one specific improvement we can mention in outreach?

Only contact a studio after identifying at least one genuine improvement opportunity.
