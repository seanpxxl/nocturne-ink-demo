# Tattoo Studio Website — Sales Playbook

## Offer in One Sentence

I build premium mobile-first websites for tattoo studios that showcase their work professionally and make it easier for potential clients to book consultations.

## First Outreach Message

Hi [Studio Name] — I came across your work and noticed your online presence could be presented much more strongly on mobile. I build premium tattoo-studio websites focused on portfolio, artists and booking. I’ve made a demo in this style and can show you what your studio could look like. Want me to send it?

## If They Say Yes

Send the demo first. Do not immediately send a wall of pricing text.

Demo:
https://seanpxxl.github.io/nocturne-ink-demo/

Then say:

This is a demo concept, so the names and photos are placeholders. For your studio I’d replace everything with your branding, artists, tattoo portfolio, WhatsApp, reviews, booking rules and domain.

## Qualification Questions

Before quoting anything beyond the standard package, identify:

- Do they already have a website?
- Do they own a domain?
- How many artists are there?
- Do they have good portfolio photography?
- How do they currently receive bookings?
- Do they need only a website form / WhatsApp or a real scheduling system?
- Who will approve the final website?

## Standard Price

Launch package: ₹10,000.

Recommended payment structure:

- ₹5,000 upfront
- ₹5,000 before production launch / handoff

Optional care plan: ₹1,500/month.

Do not discount automatically. If a first-client discount is strategically useful, reduce scope before heavily reducing price.

## What the Client Is Buying

Avoid making the pitch about HTML, CSS, GitHub or technical implementation.

Sell these outcomes:

- Stronger first impression
- Better mobile presentation
- Professional portfolio
- Artist credibility
- Easier consultation requests
- Clear contact options
- Ownership of a branded web presence instead of relying only on Instagram

## Common Objections

### “We already have Instagram.”
Instagram is great for discovery, but a website gives you a controlled presentation where visitors can see your artists, portfolio, process, FAQs and booking options without being distracted by another platform.

### “₹10,000 is expensive.”
The site is not a generic template link. It is customized with your studio identity, artists, portfolio, booking flow and domain, with mobile optimization and launch included. If needed, we can reduce scope rather than compromise the main build.

### “Can you do it for ₹5,000?”
For ₹5,000 I would need to reduce the scope significantly. The full multi-page version with artists, gallery, booking and launch is ₹10,000.

### “Can we pay after it is finished?”
I begin customization after the 50% project deposit. The balance is paid once the approved site is ready for production launch.

### “How long will it take?”
Once I have the required branding, portfolio images and studio details, the normal target is 5–7 business days.

## Follow-Up

If the studio views the demo but does not reply, follow up after 2–3 days:

Hi [Name], just checking whether you had a chance to view the tattoo studio demo I sent. If you want, I can also tell you exactly how I’d adapt the homepage and booking flow for your studio.

One more follow-up several days later is reasonable. Do not repeatedly spam a cold lead.

## Sales Rule

Do not promise functionality that has not been scoped or tested. Anything involving advanced calendars, payments, CRM automation, dashboards or custom backends must be confirmed and priced separately.
