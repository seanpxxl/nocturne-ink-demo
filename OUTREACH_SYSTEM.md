# Outreach System — Client #1

## Daily target

Start with quality over volume.

- Research 10 tattoo studios
- Qualify the best 5
- Send 5 personalized messages
- Follow up with 2–3 previous leads
- Update the tracker immediately

Once the message and offer are converting, scale gradually.

## Prospect order

Prioritize studios that:

1. Have strong tattoo work but a weak/no website.
2. Are active on Instagram.
3. Have credible reviews.
4. Have a visible phone/WhatsApp/email.
5. Look independent enough that the owner can make a decision quickly.

Avoid spending early outreach time on large chains with corporate approval layers.

## First message — Instagram / WhatsApp

Hey [Studio Name] — I came across your work and liked the [specific style / artist / piece]. I build premium mobile-first websites specifically for tattoo studios, and I recently created a Chennai-focused demo showing how a studio can present artists, work and booking in a much stronger way.

I noticed [one real observation about their current online presence]. If you’re open to it, I can send you the demo and show you what I’d improve for your studio. No pressure.

## Short version

Hey [Studio Name] — your tattoo work caught my eye, especially [specific detail]. I build premium websites for tattoo studios and made a Chennai-focused demo. I noticed [specific website/booking issue]. Can I send you the demo?

## Follow-up 1 — 2 to 4 days later

Hey, just following up on this. I’m not trying to sell you a generic template — I’d adapt the design around your actual artists, portfolio, branding and booking process. Happy to send the demo if you want to take a quick look.

## Final follow-up — about 1 week later

Last message from me on this 👍 If improving your website/booking experience becomes a priority later, I’d be happy to help. I’ll leave the demo here so you have it: https://seanpxxl.github.io/nocturne-ink-demo/

## When they ask price

The standard launch package is ₹10,000. That includes the responsive multi-page site, portfolio, artist pages, booking/contact flow, WhatsApp integration, basic SEO, mobile optimization and launch setup. I take 50% to start and the remaining 50% before launch.

If you only need a smaller scope, I can quote that separately rather than forcing you into features you don’t need.

## When they say “we already use Instagram”

Instagram is great for discovery. The website has a different job: it gives new clients one professional place to understand the studio, compare artists/styles, see the strongest work, get booking information and take action without digging through posts or DMs.

## When they say “too expensive”

Understood. I’d rather reduce the scope than promise a full custom site at a price that makes the work unsustainable. If you tell me the budget you had in mind, I can tell you what version is realistic.

## Rules

- Never fake familiarity.
- Never claim their current site is bad in an insulting way.
- Mention one genuine observation so the message is clearly personalized.
- Do not send huge paragraphs before they show interest.
- Do not discount immediately.
- Track every contact and follow-up.
