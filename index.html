06 / FAQ

Before you
book.

How much does a tattoo cost?+

Pricing depends on size, placement, detail, style and artist time. Submit a consultation brief first and the studio can estimate the project before booking.

Can I bring a Pinterest or Instagram reference?+

Yes. References are useful for direction, but the final design should be adapted into an original piece built for your body and anatomy.

Do I need to know which artist I want?+

No. If you are unsure, describe the piece and the studio can recommend the artist whose style best fits it.

Can I book through WhatsApp?+

Yes for the production version. This demo uses placeholder contact details until it is adapted for a real studio.

What should I do before my appointment?+

Sleep well, hydrate, eat beforehand and avoid arriving intoxicated. Your artist should also give you any placement-specific preparation instructions.
