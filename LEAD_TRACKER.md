## Qualification score

Score each prospect from 0–2 in each category:

1. No website / weak website
2. Strong tattoo portfolio
3. Active Instagram
4. Good reviews / reputation
5. Easy-to-find contact method
6. Looks capable of paying for a premium site
7. Clear opportunity to improve booking flow

Maximum score: 14.

Priority:
- 11–14 = A lead
- 8–10 = B lead
- 5–7 = C lead
- under 5 = low priority

## Follow-up rule

Do not spam. One initial message, one useful follow-up after 2–4 days, and one final follow-up about a week later is enough unless they engage.
